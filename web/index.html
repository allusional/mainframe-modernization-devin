CardDemo — monthly interest calculation

This page runs the same logic as the batch program CBACT04C (the mainframe INTCALC job) over the sample datasets in app/data/ASCII, entirely in your browser. No server, no framework, no build step. For each account it looks up the interest rate for the account's disclosure group and spending category, charges one month of interest (balance × annual rate ÷ 1200, truncated to cents), adds it to the balance and closes the billing cycle.

Data: app/data/ASCII (copybooks CVTRA01Y, CVACT01Y, CVACT03Y, CVTRA02Y), embedded by scripts/build-web-data.sh. Logic mirrors app/cbl/CBACT04C.cbl and its Java port in java/cbact04c; see CBACT04C-EXPLAINED.md for the business rules.
